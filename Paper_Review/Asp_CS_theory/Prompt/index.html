For a nonabelian Chern–Simons term the level quantization is standardly written $ \(\kappa = \frac{k}{4\pi}, \qquad k \in \mathbb{Z},\) $ where \(k\) is the integer level and \(\kappa\) the coupling. Writing \(\kappa = n/(4\pi)\) conflates the coupling with the topological integer and buries the actual quantization statement \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\) . This should not survive into Part 3 uncorrected.
